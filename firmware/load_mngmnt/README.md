### Setting up WiFi
1. Turn on the system
2. Wait for ~5 seconds
3. Connect to ```load_mngmt```
4. Go to your browser and type ```192.168.4.1```
5. Click on ```Configure WiFi``` - You will see a list of available WiFi networks 
6. Selectr the appropriate network
7. Enter SSID and password and click ```save```
